Scope: brokerage-fleet loads only, excluding Cancelled and anything not yet dispatched/delivered (Quoted, Open, Reserved, Covered, Dispatched, En-Route, In Transit). A load appears here if it's uninvoiced (Status isn't “Invoiced” or “Completed” — TONU loads are checked directly against invoice records instead, since TONU alone doesn't say invoiced-or-not) and/or unpaid to carrier (its Trip has no CarrierPaidAt timestamp yet). Overdue to carrier narrows that to loads past their Trip's DueDate — Alvys's own computed carrier payment due date (confirmed live: ReleasedAt + your actual term, 30 days standard or ~2 days for quickpay carriers), so most unpaid loads are simply still within normal terms, not overdue. These filters are independent, not combined. Carrier identity/name comes from the Trip, not the Load. To refresh: run python generate_report.py, then python build_dashboard.py alvys_report.json, then republish this page.
